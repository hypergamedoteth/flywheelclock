The decisions behind the numbers

Premium fees
$10.0B

net card fees, up 18%

Net card fees were $7.3B in 2023, $8.4B in 2024 and $10.0B in 2025. The average fee per card rose from $92 to $117 over two years, as more members move to fee-paying premium cards. The 10-K credits new Card Member acquisition and product refreshes.1

Rewards
43%

of revenue goes to rewards, benefits and merchant incentives

Card Member rewards 18.4B + business development 6.5B + Card Member services 6.1B = $30.9B, or 43% of net revenue (derived). That is the biggest cost, ahead of salaries ($9.0B) and marketing ($6.3B). Card Member services rose 27% on the new US Platinum benefits.1

Merchant fees
2.24%

average discount rate, falling slowly

Discount revenue was 2.24% of billed business in 2025, 2.27% in 2024 and 2.29% in 2023. Billed business grew 8% to $1,669.8B. The 10-K cites shifts in geographic and merchant spend mix.1

Interest
$17.4B

net interest income, up 12%

Interest income $25.6B less interest expense $8.2B. Growth came from revolving loan balances, partly offset by lower rates. Customer deposits rose $13.0B in the year.1

Capital return
$7.6B

returned to shareholders

$5.3B of buybacks (16.8 million shares at an average $312.87) and $2.3B of dividends, about 71% of net income to common, per the 10-K. The quarterly dividend goes from $0.82 to $0.95 starting with the Q1 2026 declaration.1
