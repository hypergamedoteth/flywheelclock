The decisions behind the numbers

Services
42%

of gross profit from 26% of revenue

Services sales were $109.2B (up 14%) with a 75.4% gross margin, against 36.8% for products. That is $82.3B of the $195.2B total gross margin (derived). The 10-K credits advertising, the App Store and cloud services for the growth.1

Buybacks
$89.3B

of stock repurchased in FY2025

402 million shares retired. In May 2025 Apple announced a new $100B program and raised the quarterly dividend from $0.25 to $0.26. Cash returned (buybacks 90.7B + dividends 15.4B) was 95% of net income (derived).1

Greater China
−4%

sales fell to $64.4B

Greater China net sales were $72.6B in FY2023, $67.0B in FY2024 and $64.4B in FY2025, now 15% of the total (derived). The 10-K attributes the FY2025 drop mainly to lower iPhone sales.1

Research and development
8.3%

of revenue goes to R&D

R&D was $34.6B, up 10%, and R&D plus SG&A ($62.2B) was 14.9% of revenue (derived). Cost of sales alone took 53%.1

Taxes
15.6%

effective tax rate, versus 24.1% a year earlier

FY2024's rate included the State Aid Decision charge. The 10-K says the provision fell $10.7B year over year on that item. Cash taxes paid were $43.4B in FY2025.1
