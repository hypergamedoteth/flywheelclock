The decisions behind the numbers

AWS
57%

of operating income from 18% of sales

AWS sales grew 20% to $128.7B at a 35.4% operating margin (derived). North America earned 7.0% and International 2.9%. Retail and AWS are different businesses in one filing.1

Capital spending
$131.8B

of capex, up from $83.0B

Purchases of property and equipment were $52.7B in 2023, $83.0B in 2024 and $131.8B in 2025. Of 2025's $142.4B total net additions, $96.5B went to AWS. Free cash flow fell from $38.2B to $11.2B.12

Advertising
$68.6B

of ad revenue, up 22%

Advertising sales grew from $46.9B in 2023 to $68.6B in 2025 (growth derived), now 9.6% of revenue (derived). The 10-K lists it as its own line.1

Third-party sellers
$172.2B

in seller services revenue

Commissions plus fulfillment and shipping fees on sellers' products were 24% of revenue (derived), second only to Amazon's own online stores ($269.3B, 38% of revenue, derived).1

One-time charges
$5.2B

of FTC settlement and severance in 2025

Operating income includes $2.5B for a settlement with the FTC and $2.7B of estimated severance costs, per the 10-K. Both are in the 2025 figures shown here.1

No payouts
$0

in buybacks 2023–2025, and no dividends

Amazon keeps its cash for investment. There were no repurchases in 2023, 2024 or 2025, and the cash flow statement has no dividend line.1
