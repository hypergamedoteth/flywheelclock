Privacy

Effective October 27, 2026. Flywheel Clock (flywheelclock.com) has no accounts, no sign-ups, no forms and no comments. We don't ask for your name or email.

What happens when you visit

The site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Like any web server, GitHub and Cloudflare process your IP address and basic browser details to deliver pages and protect the site, and may keep them in logs for security and troubleshooting. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It is privacy-focused and does not use cookies or track you across other sites. It records things like the page viewed, the referring site, browser and device type, an approximate country, and page-load performance. We see only totals.

Cookies and local storage

The site itself sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. If Cloudflare needs to check for automated traffic, it may set a strictly necessary security cookie. The live counters are calculated in your browser from numbers in the page.

Links

Source links go to other websites (government agencies, company filings, fund issuers). Those sites have their own privacy policies; nothing is sent to them unless you click through.

Not advice

Flywheel Clock is for information only. Live counters are projections from annual figures, not real-time data. Nothing here is investment advice, and we are not affiliated with any company or fund shown.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
