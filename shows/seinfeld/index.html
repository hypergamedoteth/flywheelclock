Why the ownership paid off

NBC aired the show. NBC did not own the reruns.

From 1970, fin-syn rules kept the big networks from owning the syndication of shows they licensed from studios. NBC could broadcast Seinfeld. The reruns stayed with the studio. That is the account in the Acquired episode. 1

Castle Rock owned the show. In 1993 Turner bought Castle Rock. The LA Times reported about $100 million in cash and about $100 million of debt assumed. Sony owned 44% of Castle Rock and kept the syndication rights in perpetuity, so each renewal was a sale. 20

It is reported that Jerry Seinfeld and Larry David each held 15% of the show. The terms were never published. Acquired uses that figure. It is an estimate, not a filing. 1
