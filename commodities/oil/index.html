EIA's 2024 world production of petroleum and other liquids was 103,164 thousand barrels a day, so about 1,194 barrels a second (103,164,470 ÷ 86,400). Consumption was 103,110 thousand a day. "Petroleum and other liquids" includes crude oil, natural gas liquids, biofuels and refinery gain, so it is larger than crude oil alone (81,958 thousand a day). The counters are a 2024 daily average projected forward, not live production. The dollar line uses the 2025 average Brent spot price, not a live price. 2 3

Who holds the reserves

Proved reserves are oil that is known to exist and can be recovered under current technology and prices. OPEC's 2026 bulletin counts 1,572.5 billion barrels worldwide at the end of 2025. Here is who holds them, one square per 10 billion barrels.
